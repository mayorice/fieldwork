Privacy Policy for Fieldwork

Last updated: 25 September 2026

Fieldwork respects your privacy. Fieldwork does not collect, transmit, sell, or share personal information or user data.

Data Collection

Fieldwork does not collect personal information or user data. Information created or entered in the app is stored locally on the user's device and is not transmitted to the developer or third parties.

Analytics and Tracking

Fieldwork does not use advertising, analytics, or tracking technologies to monitor users.

Third-Party Data Sharing

Fieldwork does not sell or share user information with third parties.

Data Retention and Deletion

The developer does not retain user data on external servers. Locally stored app data can be removed by deleting the relevant data within the app, where available, or by deleting the app from the device.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

If Fieldwork's data practices change, this Privacy Policy will be updated to reflect those changes.

Contact

For privacy questions, use the developer contact details listed on Fieldwork's App Store page.
